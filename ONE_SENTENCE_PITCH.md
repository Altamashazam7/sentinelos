# SentinelOS One-Sentence Pitches

- **Investor:** SentinelOS is building an Autonomous Engineering Platform that
  connects production incidents to evidence-backed, testable, reviewable
  software changes.
- **Judge:** SentinelOS takes a real failure signal, traces its evidence,
  proposes a repair with regression coverage, and shows whether validation
  actually passed.
- **Enterprise:** SentinelOS helps engineering teams operationalize incident
  evidence into controlled repair workflows with explicit validation and human
  approval.
- **Technical:** SentinelOS orchestrates repository and incident evidence,
  model-generated diagnosis and patches, regression-test generation, sandbox
  validation, and an approval-gated GitHub change path.

## Short tagline

**From production signal to verified change.**
